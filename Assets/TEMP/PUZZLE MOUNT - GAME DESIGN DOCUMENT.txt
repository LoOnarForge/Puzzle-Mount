PUZZLE MOUNT - GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT
Game Overview
Grid-based puzzle platformer set in South American temple ruins. Player controls Tim, an explorer who pushes and rotates cubes to connect power lines from sources to targets. Combines precise cube manipulation with platforming elements in an Indiana Jones-inspired setting.
Core Mechanics
Cube Types
Basic Cube - Standard moveable and rotatable block with power lines
Heavy Cube - Can only be rotated, cannot be pushed
Static Cube - Fixed environmental obstacles, cannot move or rotate
Power Source - Generates power, cannot be moved, feeds power into connected lines
Power Mixer - Combines different colored power types into new colors
Power Target - End goal that must receive power (switches, gates, devices)
Power Line Types
Straight Line - Connects two opposite faces
L-Corner - 90-degree connection between adjacent faces
T-Junction - Three-way connection with one input, two outputs
Cross - Four-way connection, all directions connected
Power System
Directional Flow - Power always flows away from sources
Color Types - Multiple power colors (Red, Blue, Yellow, etc.)
Power Mixing - Different colors combine to create new colors
Face Independence - Each cube face operates separately unless internally connected
Surface Types
Normal - Standard grip and movement
Sticky - Locks cubes in place when contacted
Slippery - Cubes slide until hitting obstacle, player cannot stop mid-slide
Brittle - Surface breaks after 1-3 cube pushes over it
Pressure Plates - Activated by cube weight, trigger mechanisms
Environmental Mechanics
Bridges - Built from collected wood pieces, span gaps and chasms
Cranes/Elevators - Move cubes to higher levels, require collected materials
Moving Platforms - Power-activated temporary paths
Switches/Gates - Opened by powered connections
Chasms - Can be filled with unwanted cubes to create crossing paths
Player Actions
Tim's Movement
Walk - Precise movement for accurate cube positioning
Run - Faster movement with slight reach extension
Jump - Basic platforming over obstacles
Cube Manipulation
Push - Move cube stacks (maximum 3 cubes) in cardinal directions
Rotate - Turn cubes 90 degrees to align power lines (Q/E keys)
Tab Selection - Cycle through available cubes in stack at Tim's level
Push Timing System
Initial Delay - 0.35 seconds before first push to prevent accidental moves
Continuous Delay - 0.1 seconds between subsequent pushes for precision control
Contact Required - Must maintain contact with cube to continue pushing sequence
Environmental Variety
Lava Zones - Hot surfaces, dramatic lighting, ember effects
Cave Interior - Dark atmosphere, ancient temple architecture
Icy Areas - Slippery surfaces, snow effects, cold themes
Forest Ruins - Overgrown elements, natural lighting, wooden structures
Resource Collection & Building
Wood Pieces - Collected in levels, used for bridge construction
Metal Parts - Required for crane and elevator mechanisms
Rope - Needed for rope bridges and pulley systems
No Global Resources - All materials found in the level where they're needed
Progression System
Environmental Complexity - New mechanics introduced gradually across levels
Visual Camp - Vehicle assembly shows progress, decorative collectibles
Vehicle Escape - Collect parts to build escape vehicle as ultimate goal
Game Modes
Story Mode - Standard puzzle progression
Limited Moves - Complete level with maximum 50 cube pushes
Timed Challenges - Speed-based puzzle solving
Technical Details
Grid System - All movement and placement on 1-meter grid
Visual-Only Rotation - Cube mesh rotates while collider stays grid-aligned
Raycast Detection - Precise cube targeting and interaction
Child Object Lines - Each power line as separate GameObject for individual control
